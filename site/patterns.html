Patterns

Fourteen patterns for mixed-initiative, tool-using interfaces.

I · Orientation

  • 1 Capability Disclosure Make the agent’s powers, position and limits legible before the principal commits to a request.
  • 2 Mode Legibility Make the active mode visible at the moment of use, not merely settable at the moment of configuration.

II · Turn

  • 3 Streaming Turn Emit the response as it is produced, so the principal’s wait overlaps the agent’s work instead of following it.
  • 4 Barge-In Let the principal seize the turn at any moment — and define honestly what that stops.

III · Intent

  • 5 Scope-on-Uncertainty Ask one question when ambiguity would change the blast radius; otherwise proceed on a stated assumption.
  • 6 Constrained Choice When ambiguity is enumerable, offer the options as selectable structure rather than inviting free text.

IV · Execution

  • 7 Plan · Approve · Execute · Receipt Four phases, so that understanding, consent, action and evidence are each separately inspectable.
  • 8 Tool-Call Transparency Expose which tool ran, against what target, why, and with what result — as structured events, not prose.
  • 9 Ambient Activity Channel Signal state through a channel the principal perceives without attending to it.

V · Authority

  • 10 Approval Gate Explicit, per-action consent before anything irreversible, outward-facing, or that spends the principal’s authority.
  • 11 Progressive Autonomy Let the scope of unattended action widen with demonstrated reliability and narrow with risk.

VI · Truth and continuity

  • 12 Honest Gauge A signal must be wrong only when the system is wrong. One that reads red in health tells you nothing on the day it is right.
  • 13 Recoverable Execution Every action has a visible before-state and a path back, so that being wrong is cheap.
  • 14 Continuity as Memory Carry what matters in durable, correctable memory rather than in the length of a transcript.
Each pattern is stated in full — intent, motivation, structure, consequences, failure signature — in OM-001, Conversational Agent Control Surfaces. Also there: the acceptance checklist, the recurring forces, and what the catalogue does not cover.
