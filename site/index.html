Ambient Signals7

the peripheral channel · OM-004

Not a rung on the ladder, and drawn beside it rather than below it. The column is focal and single-channel: a turn is taken, attended to, and answered. This is the peripheral alternative — it reports when no turn is in progress, and there may never be one. Stacked as a sixth level it would read as a finer grain of the same thing. It is not. It runs alongside every one of them.
